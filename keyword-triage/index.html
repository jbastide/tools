How verdicts are decided

  • Cut: spent past your threshold with zero conversions, or CPA is over 2× target (ROAS under half of target). These lose money.
  • Trim bids: converting, but 20–100% over target CPA (or ROAS 50–83% of target). Lower bids or tighten the match type before cutting.
  • Keep: on target, give or take 20%. Also good performers that don't have enough conversions yet to be sure.
  • Scale: at least 20% better than target with enough conversions to trust. If the report includes Search lost IS, it says whether budget or bids are holding the keyword back.
  • Needs data: not enough clicks or spend to call yet. Leave these running.

These are starting points, not autopilot. Check the conversion lag, seasonality and assisted conversions before pausing anything big.
