Google Ads for law firms: five short answers

Short answers to the five Google Ads questions law firms ask us most: what a good conversion rate is, how many conversions Target CPA needs, whether to use broad match, which negative keywords to add, and whether to use Maximize conversions or Target CPA. Each answer is for lead gen Search campaigns.

By Copy for Originals · Published October 5, 2026 · Last updated October 5, 2026

What is a good conversion rate for law firm Google Ads?

A good conversion rate for law firm Google Ads is one that hits the firm’s target cost per lead at its own cost per click. The rate needed is cost per click ÷ target cost per lead. A firm paying $100 a click that can afford $2,000 a lead needs 5% of clicks to call or submit a form.

That makes “good” a number you can calculate from your own account instead of a benchmark from someone else’s. If your rate is below what your target needs, fix the landing page and the keywords before raising the budget.

How many conversions does Target CPA need?

Google recommends judging Target CPA performance over periods with at least 30 conversions. Below that, run Maximize conversions without a target, then add a target CPA equal to your actual cost per lead once you have about 30 conversions.

Change the target by no more than about 15% at a time, so the bid strategy doesn’t restart learning.

Should law firms use broad match keywords?

Only with a target CPA or ROAS and at least 50 conversions a month, in our experience. Below that, broad match has too little data to tell a good search from a loose one, and law firm clicks are too expensive to spend on loose matches. Start with phrase and exact match.

From experience

Broad match doesn’t have to be bad. With a target and enough conversions, it can find good searches you didn’t think of. Check the search terms report weekly either way.

What negative keywords should a law firm use?

Start with words that signal someone who isn’t a potential client, then add negatives weekly from the search terms report.

Be careful with “free”: many injury firms offer a free consultation, and “free consultation” searches can be good leads. Add it as a negative only if those searches don’t convert for you.

Should a law firm use Maximize conversions or Target CPA?

Start with Maximize conversions until the campaign has about 30 conversions, then add a target CPA equal to the actual cost per lead. Without a target, Maximize conversions tries to spend the whole daily budget whatever each lead costs, so watch cost per lead closely in the first weeks.

Limitations

  • These answers are for lead gen Search campaigns, not Performance Max or Local Services Ads.
  • The broad match threshold is our experience, not a Google requirement.
  • Check your state bar’s advertising rules separately; this page covers Google Ads settings only.
