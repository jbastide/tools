Which parts of digital marketing does Copy for Originals work with?

Copy for Originals works with three parts of digital marketing: search ads in Google Ads, search engine optimization (SEO) and the copy that gets visitors to get in touch. Social media advertising and web builds are not offered at the moment.

Digital marketing is all marketing in digital channels. For a company that lives on inquiries, search ads are often the fastest way to find out whether it pays, because every krona can be followed from search to inquiry. A comparison of the channels is on the page about Google Ads for lead generation, and the way of working for search engine optimization is described on the page about SEO and AEO.

Are there free tools for Google Ads?

Yes. Copy for Originals has built free tools for Google Ads that run entirely in the browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. The tools are available in English and Swedish.

  • Negative Keyword Finder: reads a search terms report and finds the searches that cost money without bringing inquiries.
  • Keyword Triage: sorts the keywords into cut, trim bids, keep and scale, against what you can pay for an inquiry.
  • PPC Troubleshooter: diagnoses clicks without inquiries, ads that don’t show and results that suddenly drop.
  • Target CPA & ROAS Calculator: works out what an inquiry may cost from your margin and how many inquiries become customers.
  • Lead Gen Search Campaign Checklist: a checklist for Search campaigns, setting by setting.
  • PPC Gumball Rule: how many clicks without an inquiry is bad luck, and when something is wrong.
