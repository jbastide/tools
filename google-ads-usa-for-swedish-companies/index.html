How is Google Ads in the US different from Google Ads in Sweden?

Google Ads works the same way technically in the US and in Sweden, but six things differ: the language, the geography, the click prices, the time zones, the buyer’s expectations and the rules. Anyone who runs a translated Swedish campaign at the whole United States gets all six working against them at once.

Where in the US should a Swedish company start advertising?

Start in one or two states or metro areas, not the whole United States. A test budget spread over fifty states produces too few clicks in any one place to show anything. Pick the region where you already have customers, a distributor or a warehouse, and where someone can answer during office hours.

  • The East Coast gives the most overlap. Office hours in New York start at 3 PM Swedish time, so a Swedish office that closes at 5 PM can answer for two hours.
  • The location option. Switch from “Presence or interest”, which is Google’s default, to “Presence”. With the default, the ads can also be shown to people who have only shown interest in the area. With “Presence”, they are shown to people who are likely to be located there, or regularly are.
  • One service, one page. One campaign for what you most want to sell in the US, with its own landing page, tells you more than five campaigns sharing the same budget.

How much budget does it take to test Google Ads in the US?

A test budget for Google Ads in the US has to buy enough clicks that zero inquiries can no longer be bad luck, which is three times the expected cost per inquiry. Cost per inquiry is cost per click divided by conversion rate. For a high-competition service business, Copy for Originals plans on a test budget of $5,000 to $10,000.

Who answers when an American customer gets in touch at 9 PM Swedish time?

That has to be decided before the campaign starts, because 9 PM in Sweden is the middle of the working day in the US. In a Harvard Business Review study of 1.25 million sales leads at 42 US companies, firms that tried to contact the customer within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to have a qualifying conversation as those that waited another hour, and more than 60 times as likely as those that waited 24 hours or longer.

There are four ways to solve it, and one of them should be chosen before the first click:

  • Show the ads only when someone answers. The ad schedule is limited to the hours you are available.
  • Collect forms instead of calls. The inquiry is answered so the reply is in the customer’s inbox when they start work the next day.
  • Let the customer book a time. A booking link that shows open slots in the customer’s own time zone.
  • Have someone answer in the US. A distributor, an employee or an answering service.

How are inquiries from the US measured?

Inquiries from the US are measured as submitted forms and phone calls in Google Ads, and then supplemented with what happened next: which inquiries were qualified and which became customers. That information is in your own system and can be imported into Google Ads as offline conversions, so bidding learns which searches lead to business.

  • Calls. Google forwarding numbers, which are used to measure calls from ads, are available in both the US and Sweden according to Google.
  • Qualified inquiries. We write down what counts as a qualified inquiry before the campaign starts. Otherwise it ends in a discussion about whether the inquiries were good or bad.
  • Offline conversions. Google describes enhanced conversions for leads as an upgraded version of offline conversion import.
  • The measure. Cost per qualified inquiry and cost per new customer, not cost per click.

FAQ

Do we need a US company to advertise on Google in the US?

No, not for the advertising. A Swedish company can target its Google Ads at locations in the US from its own account. Questions about company structure, tax and liability in the US are for you to settle with your accountant and lawyer.

Should we use our Swedish Google Ads account or open a new one for the US?

The currency and time zone of a Google Ads account are set when the account is created and, according to Google, can’t be changed afterwards. If you want budgets and reports in dollars and in US time, you need a new account. Otherwise a separate US campaign in the existing account works fine.

What time of day should the ads run in the US?

When someone can answer. If inquiries are answered from Sweden during Swedish working hours, the overlap with the East Coast is about two hours, 3 PM to 5 PM Swedish time. Campaigns that collect forms instead of calls can run through the whole American working day, if the reply is in the customer’s inbox when they start work the next day.

Do you handle both the ads and the landing page copy?

Yes. Copy for Originals writes the ads and the landing page copy in American English and runs the campaigns. Then one person is responsible for the whole path from search to inquiry.

Do you promise a certain number of inquiries from the US?

No. Results depend on the competition in the ad auction, on the offer and on the landing page. What I promise is to work out what an inquiry may cost before we spend anything, and to turn off what isn’t working early.
