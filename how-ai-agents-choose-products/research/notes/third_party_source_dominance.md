# Third-Party Source Dominance in AI Product Recommendations: The "Citation Economy" (2025-2026)

Scope note: Nearly every large-scale citation dataset in this area is produced by a vendor that sells AI-visibility tooling (Profound, Semrush, Ahrefs, Yext, Otterly, AirOps/Foundation, Muck Rack, Suff Digital, Wix Studio, Overthink Group/Amadora). Their incentives are flagged per finding. Peer-reviewed/academic audits exist but are smaller and mostly non-commercial in query scope. Dates are given for every statistic because the shares move substantially month to month.

## KQ1: What do large-scale citation studies find? Which domains top the lists per platform?

### Takeaway
Across 2025 studies, a small set of third-party UGC and reference domains (Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube, LinkedIn, Quora) plus a handful of editorial/affiliate publishers (Forbes, Business Insider, TechRadar, NerdWallet, PCMag) and review aggregators (G2, Gartner, Yelp, TripAdvisor) dominate the top-10 lists on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode and Perplexity; but the top-10 collectively is only a small slice of total citations (e.g., Wikipedia at 7.8% of all ChatGPT citations was the single largest), and by late 2026 Ahrefs data shows ChatGPT's Reddit and Wikipedia shares collapsed by 80-88% in favor of primary sources, government bodies, brand sites and hands-on testers.

### Cited Findings
- Profound "AI Platform Citation Patterns" (published June 5, 2025; marked updated Aug 2025): ~680 million citations, Aug 2024-June 2025, ChatGPT / Google AI Overviews / Perplexity. ChatGPT top 10 by share of ALL citations: Wikipedia 7.8%, Reddit 1.8%, Forbes 1.1%, G2 1.1%, TechRadar 0.9%, NerdWallet 0.8%, Business Insider 0.8%, NY Post 0.7%, Toxigon 0.7%, Reuters 0.6%. Google AI Overviews: Reddit 2.2%, YouTube 1.9%, Quora 1.5%, LinkedIn 1.3%, Gartner 0.7%, NerdWallet 0.6%, Forbes 0.6%, Wikipedia 0.6%, Business Insider 0.5%, Medium 0.4%. Perplexity: Reddit 6.6%, YouTube 2.0%, Gartner 1.0%, Yelp 0.8%, LinkedIn 0.8%, Forbes 0.7%, NerdWallet 0.6%, TripAdvisor 0.6%, G2 0.6%, PCMag 0.5%. Profound also reports "top source share" (share within the top-10 only), which is where the widely quoted "Wikipedia = 47.9% of ChatGPT" and "Reddit = 46.7% of Perplexity" figures come from; these are NOT shares of all citations. 80.4% of ChatGPT citations went to .com TLDs. No commercial-query split, no brand-owned vs third-party split, no time series. Vendor: Profound sells AI-visibility software. — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns)
- Secondary summaries frequently misreport the Profound top-source-share numbers as overall shares (e.g., "Reddit ~21%, YouTube 18.8% of AI Overviews"), so figures in the 20-47% range for a single domain should be read as share-of-top-10. — [Search Engine Land summary](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138); [Decoding](https://trydecoding.com/blog/top-cited-domains-in-ai/)
- Semrush 3-month study (published Nov 10, 2025): 230,000+ prompts, weekly snapshots July 14-Oct 12, 2025, ChatGPT search / Google AI Mode / Perplexity, 100M+ citations, top 25 domains per week. ChatGPT: Wikipedia and Reddit were the two most-cited domains and, before September, were cited ~5x as often as the next tier (Medium, Forbes, LinkedIn). Google AI Mode: LinkedIn (~15% of responses), YouTube, Reddit, Google, Google Blog; Wikipedia only ~2-3%. Perplexity: Reddit, LinkedIn, NIH, Microsoft, Google; Wikipedia ~0.8%. Reddit and LinkedIn were top-5 on all three platforms. AI Mode shows a clear preference for Google-owned properties (Google, Google Blog, YouTube). Vendor: Semrush. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai/)
- Semrush June 2025 snapshot (~150,000 citations, 5,000 keywords) reported Reddit 40.1%, Wikipedia 26.3%, YouTube 23.5% of responses including those domains (response-level share, not citation-level). — [DesignRush on Semrush](https://news.designrush.com/reddit-40-percent-of-ai-generated-responses-2025)
- Semrush AI Visibility Index (Sept 3, 2025; five industries incl. consumer electronics; prompt count not disclosed): Wikipedia #1 or #2 cited source in four of five verticals (167% citation frequency in ChatGPT digital-tech responses, i.e., cited more than once per answer on average). Reddit heavily cited by ChatGPT: 176.9% in finance, 127.3% in consumer electronics. G2 was the 4th most-cited source in digital tech (20.0% in ChatGPT). Google AI Mode favored comparison sites in finance (Bankrate 86.6%, NerdWallet 75.1%). "Official company websites rarely appear in top source lists"; Microsoft's corporate blog earned fewer citations than Reddit threads about Microsoft products. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-study-findings/)
- Ahrefs (Oct 2026), based on Brand Radar US ChatGPT citations March-Sept 2026 (2.4-3.8M answers/month) plus 66.6M logged-out GPT-5.6 responses (Aug-Sept 2026): Reddit fell from ~1 in 5 ChatGPT answers in March 2026 to ~1 in 43 by late Sept (-88%); Wikipedia fell from 1 in 7 to 1 in 35 (-80%); YouTube from 1 in 370 to 1 in 500 (-27%). PubMed rose from 64th to 4th most-cited domain; FDA entered at 8th. Rising: government/regulators, professional bodies, brand sites and support pages, hands-on testers (RTINGS, CarComplaints, RepairPal). Falling: health/finance explainers (WebMD, Healthline, Verywell), encyclopedias, syndicators/tabloids (Yahoo, AOL), low-quality content sites, marketplaces (Alibaba). Same direction held on ~25,000 prompts present in both March and Sept. Only 14.4% of ChatGPT responses triggered a web search at all. Vendor: Ahrefs. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt)
- Academic audit (Allaham & Diakopoulos, AIES 2026, arXiv May 22, 2026): 712 real human queries (politics, health, environment) across ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity. Engines "repeatedly cite a fairly narrow set of domains" while surfacing a long tail cited minimally; ~16% of cited sources showed evidence of being AI-generated. Not commercial queries. — [arXiv 2605.23684](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23684)
- Semrush "ghost citations" study with Kevin Indig (June 2026): 3,981 domain appearances across 115 prompts, 14 countries, ChatGPT / AI Overviews / Gemini / AI Mode. 61.7% of appearances were "ghost citations" (domain linked as a source, brand never named in the answer); 13.2% both cited and mentioned; 25.1% mentioned without citation. ChatGPT cited domains in 87% of appearances but named the brand in only 20.7%; Gemini named brands in 83.7% but cited a source only 21.4% of the time. — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/blog/the-ghost-citations-study/)

### Inferences
- The "who dominates" answer is platform-specific and time-specific: Perplexity and Google AI Overviews have been structurally Reddit/YouTube/UGC-heavy; ChatGPT was Wikipedia/Reddit-heavy in 2025 but by autumn 2026 tilted toward primary and official sources. Any 2025 top-10 list is already stale as a description of ChatGPT.
- Even at the peak, no single domain exceeded ~8% of all ChatGPT citations (Profound) — the citation economy is a long tail with a fat head, so "being on Reddit/Wikipedia" is a necessary-looking but not sufficient condition.
- Because a majority of citations (61.7%) are ghost citations and most ChatGPT answers (85.6%) do not trigger web search at all, citation share is a loose proxy for recommendation share.

### Gaps
- No study found that publishes per-platform top-10 lists for Claude or Microsoft Copilot specifically; Otterly's licensing study covered 7 platforms including Claude and Copilot but only published aggregated licensing comparisons.
- Semrush did not publish its full per-domain percentage tables in text form; only the narrative figures above are available.

## KQ2: For product/commercial-intent queries, how often do Wirecutter, Consumer Reports, RTINGS, Reddit, YouTube, Amazon, Forbes, CNET, Tom's Guide, TechRadar, G2 appear? Is there data isolating "best X" queries?

### Takeaway
On "best X" prompts, third-party listicles and affiliate review publishers are the dominant cited format: Wix Studio found listicles took 40.9% of commercial-intent citations (vs 21.9% overall), and a Sept 2026 study of 17,524 answers to 2,000 "best product" questions found 70% cited an affiliate review site (Perplexity 99.4%, AI Overviews 79.3%, AI Mode 70.1%, ChatGPT 61%, Gemini 54.7%), with Forbes (14.4%), Wirecutter (10.3%), Consumer Reports (7.6%), TechRadar (7.3%), CNET (7.2%), Tom's Guide (7.2%) and RTINGS (7.0%) the most frequent; in B2B software, 70.8% of citations for "best [category]" prompts carried "best/top" in the title and the G2 network took 8%.

### Cited Findings
- Wix Studio AI Search Lab: 75,000 AI answers, 1,056,727 citations, ChatGPT / Google AI Mode / Perplexity (2025). Overall: listicles 21.9%, articles 16.7%, product pages 13.7% of citations (>50% together). Commercial-intent queries: listicles 40.9% ("nearly double other intents"). Informational: articles 45.5%, listicles 21.7%. Query intent was the strongest predictor of cited content type, across industries and models. In professional services, third-party listicles were 80.9% of listicle citations vs 19.1% for self-promotional (brand-authored) lists. Observational; does not isolate format from authority. — [Wix Studio](https://wix.com/studio/ai-search-lab/research/content-types-most-cited-by-llms); summarized by [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-citations-favor-listicles-articles-product-pages-study-472364)
- Suff Digital (vendor/agency; run Sept 23, 2026): 17,524 answers to 2,000 "best product or service" questions in 20 shopping categories across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews. 70% of answers cited at least one affiliate review site. Per engine: Perplexity 99.4%, AI Overviews 79.3%, AI Mode 70.1%, ChatGPT 61%, Gemini 54.7%. Share of all answers citing: Forbes 14.4%, Wirecutter 10.3%, Consumer Reports 7.6%, TechRadar 7.3%, CNET 7.2%, Tom's Guide 7.2%, RTINGS 7.0%. Reddit, YouTube, Amazon and brand sites not reported; prompt wording not published. — [Suff Digital](https://www.suffdigital.com/resources/data-studies/ai-product-answers-affiliate-sites)
- Ahrefs (Oct 2026) running-shoes sample of ChatGPT fan-out queries: the word "best" appeared 206 times, "review" 117 times and "official" only 65 times, implying ChatGPT actively searches for third-party best-of and review content more than for official pages in that category. "Price" fan-outs rose from 6.04% to 8.88% of queries in one month; "reviews" fan-outs grew ~1/3; "specifications" entered the top 50. ChatGPT also now cites hands-on testers (RTINGS, CarComplaints, RepairPal) more. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt)
- Overthink Group / Amadora B2B SaaS study (June 2026, published July 14, 2026): 1,260-1,263 prompts across 250 G2 software categories, four templates incl. "What is the best [category]?", ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity / AI Overviews, one business week. 70.8% of all citations had "best/top" language in the title; 51.6% included "2026". Review aggregators (Gartner, G2, Capterra, TrustRadius) ~8.6% of citations; G2 network 8.0% (G2.com 5.8%), with Perplexity supplying >71% of G2's citations. Reddit only 1.4% (7th). YouTube was the top domain in AI Overviews (7.4% of AIO citations) but <1% overall. Spam/programmatic sites ~8.6% overall: >14% of ChatGPT citations, ~11% of Perplexity, 0.16% of AI Overviews, 0.46% of Gemini. Among the top-100 domains per platform, ~3/4 were vendors in AIOs and Gemini, but ~3/4 were NOT vendors in ChatGPT and Perplexity. — [Overthink Group](https://overthinkgroup.com/b2b-ai-citation-stats-2026-q2/)
- Semrush Reddit study (Nov 10, 2025; 217,000 prompts, 248,000 Reddit URLs, data refreshed Oct 2025): Reddit was the #1 cited domain on Perplexity (~4% of answers), #2 on ChatGPT search (13%), #3 on Google AI Mode (9%). Q&A threads were >50% of cited Reddit posts; "comparison" posts framing product/tool evaluations were the second most-cited format. Cited posts were low-engagement (median 5-8 upvotes, ~80% under 20 upvotes), ~900 days old on average, median ~80 words. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddit-ai-search-visibility-study/)
- Semrush AI Visibility Index (Sept 2025): consumer electronics answers named only 1.22 (ChatGPT) and 1.36 (AI Mode) brands per answer on average; Samsung 58.1%, Apple 48.8%, Google 38.5% share of voice. The article hypothesizes comparison queries ("best CRM software") draw on Reddit and review sites while factual queries (pricing, features) draw on official sites. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-study-findings/)
- Profound's overall ChatGPT top-10 (Aug 2024-June 2025) includes G2 (1.1%), TechRadar (0.9%), NerdWallet (0.8%); Perplexity's includes Gartner, Yelp, TripAdvisor, G2, PCMag — all third-party review/aggregator sites — but Profound did not isolate commercial queries. — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns)
- RTINGS moved its full test results behind a paywall in March 2026, citing decreasing Google traffic and "uncredited scraping from AI" (per Wikipedia's summary of RTINGS' statement). — [Wikipedia: RTINGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTINGS)

### Inferences
- The most-cited product reviewers in AI answers are the same affiliate-economy publishers that dominate Google's "best X" SERPs (Forbes Vetted, Wirecutter, CNET, Tom's Guide, TechRadar), plus the independent testers (Consumer Reports, RTINGS). Forbes outranking Wirecutter in Sept 2026 is consistent with Semrush noting Forbes doubled its ChatGPT citation presence after the Sept 2025 shift.
- Reddit's weight for product queries is platform-dependent: large on Perplexity and (in 2025) ChatGPT, small in B2B software (1.4%) and in Yext's local/branded dataset (2%).
- Perplexity is the most third-party-dependent engine for shopping queries (99.4% of "best product" answers cite an affiliate site); Gemini and Google AI Overviews are the most vendor/brand-site-friendly.

### Gaps
- No study was found that isolates Amazon or YouTube citation rates specifically for consumer "best X" queries with published percentages (Suff Digital did not report them; Overthink is B2B).
- The Suff Digital study is a single-day snapshot from an agency with no published prompt list; treat the per-publisher percentages as indicative.

## KQ3: What share of citations go to the brand's own domain for branded vs unbranded queries? Does owned content get cited for detail while third parties decide the shortlist?

### Takeaway
The data split sharply by query type: brand-owned domains receive roughly 30-78% of citations on branded prompts and ~2-15% on unbranded "best X" discovery prompts (AirOps/Foundation, 57M citations), while Ahrefs finds official sites are 63.4% of citations for factual questions but 46.4% for comparison questions; Yext's "86% brand-managed" headline comes from local, listings-heavy queries and is not contradictory once intent is controlled.

### Cited Findings
- AirOps + Foundation (data Dec 2025-Feb 2026, 60 days; 50 B2B brands, 7 verticals, 5.1M responses, 57.2M citations, five unnamed AI platforms): brand-owned share of citations was 30.7% on branded prompts vs 2.24% on unbranded prompts (~13.7x); at response level, 77.6% of branded responses contained at least one brand-owned citation vs 15.3% of unbranded; 85% of unbranded responses cited no brand-owned source at all. Overall 10.15% of citations went to brand-owned domains. Third-party sources cited instead: review sites, Reddit threads, LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, competitor pages. Vendor: AirOps sells AI-visibility tooling. — [Foundation Inc](https://foundationinc.co/lab/branded-unbranded-ai-citations)
- Ahrefs (Oct 2026) 20,000 US ChatGPT prompts split factual vs comparison: factual questions (pricing, features, company details) cited at least one official source 84.4% of the time vs 68.3% for comparison questions; official websites were 63.4% of citations for factual questions and 46.4% for comparisons. Brand `site:` searches made up 75.9% of ChatGPT fan-out searches to the ~160 most-searched domains (retailers 11.9%; Reddit+Facebook 1.9%); 8 of the top-10 `site:`-searched domains were consumer brands (apple.com 9,442; ikea.com 7,361; nike.com 5,210; adidas.com 4,619; samsung.com 4,432; mi.com; lego.com; dior.com). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt)
- Yext (press release Oct 9, 2025): 6.8M citations, 1.6M queries per model, ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity, July 1-Aug 31, 2025, retail / financial services / healthcare / food service, from Yext client/prospect locations. First-party websites 44%, listings 42%, reviews/social 8% (= 86% "brand-managed"); Reddit and forums ~2% once location and intent were applied. Gemini favored websites (52.1%); OpenAI leaned on listings (48.7%); Perplexity spread across MapQuest, TripAdvisor etc. Unbranded objective queries drew nearly 60% of citations from first-party websites and local pages; branded or subjective queries leaned more on listings and reviews. Vendor: Yext sells listings management; sample is its own customers' local queries. — [Yext](https://www.yext.com/about/news-media/ai-citations-release)
- Muck Rack "What is AI Reading?" (May 2026 edition): 25M+ links from ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini across 17 industries; earned media 84% of citations (range 82-89% across editions since July 2025); journalism 25-27%; paid/advertorial 0.3%. Vendor: Muck Rack sells PR software; "earned media" is defined broadly (any non-owned, non-paid third-party page). — [Muck Rack](https://muckrack.com/blog/what-is-ai-reading-may-2026)
- Ranqo (company-authored arXiv preprint, 2026, GEO measurement): reports ~78% of citations go to corporate websites and ranked best-of listicles are the most-cited content format (~21%); broad discovery questions surface a brand most (~23%) and specific use-case questions least (~11%). — [arXiv 2606.20065](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.20065)
- Kern study (5,376 citations, small sample): 84% pointed to company websites but on-site "best of" comparison pages were only 1.7% of citations; third-party best-of listicles dominated non-company citations. — [Obsurfable summary](https://obsurfable.com/resources/articles/ai-search-cites-vendor-pages-not-blogs-kern-study)
- Tannenbaum (arXiv preprint 2026, 34,960 unbranded prompt observations): when neither the target brand nor its domain appeared in the observable retrieval path, the target brand was mentioned in 2.8% (GPT) / 3.8% (Gemini) of answers; with an own-domain citation present but no branded fan-out, mention rates rose to 49.0% / 58.4%. Author cautions this is observational. — [arXiv 2609.23162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23162)
- Semrush ghost-citation study: only 6-27% of the most-mentioned brands were also top cited sources (Sept 2025 index); Zapier was the #1 cited source in digital tech but #44 in brand mentions — citations and recommendations diverge. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-study-findings/)

### Inferences
- The studies reporting 78-86% brand-owned/vendor citations (Yext, Ranqo, Kern) sample branded, local, or factual prompts, or B2B vendor-heavy categories; the studies reporting 2-15% brand-owned (AirOps/Foundation unbranded; Overthink ChatGPT/Perplexity) sample unbranded "best X" discovery prompts. Read together, they support the two-stage pattern: third-party editorial/UGC content shapes the shortlist on discovery prompts, and owned pages are fetched for specs, prices and support once a brand is named.
- Ahrefs' finding that ChatGPT runs `site:brand.com` searches heavily, and cites official sources 68% of the time even on comparisons, means owned content is not irrelevant to discovery; it is retrieved to verify details about brands already in the candidate set.
- Tannenbaum's data (2.8% mention rate without any retrieval-path presence vs ~50% with own-domain citation) is the closest thing to a quantified "absence" penalty, but it cannot distinguish cause from selection.

### Gaps
- No consumer-category (as opposed to B2B) study was found that publishes brand-owned citation share split by branded vs unbranded prompts; AirOps/Foundation is B2B, Yext is local.
- Seer Interactive's 541,213-response study (claimed to show the model picks brands from parametric memory first, then finds links) was only found in secondary summaries; primary not retrieved.

## KQ4: Is there measured correlation between off-site mention volume and AI appearance rate ("brand mentions are the new backlinks")?

### Takeaway
Yes, with the usual correlation-not-causation caveat: Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study found branded web mentions had the strongest Spearman correlation with AI Overview brand visibility (0.664) versus referring domains (0.295), Domain Rating (0.326) and backlinks (0.218); Seer Interactive and Kevin Indig found similar mid-range correlations for ChatGPT, and domain authority is a weak gate (47.9% of 20.5M citations went to DA<40 domains).

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs (May 26, 2025; 75,000 brands with DR>40 and top keyword volume >=800; Google AI Overviews via Brand Radar; Spearman): branded web mentions 0.664; branded anchors 0.527; branded search volume 0.392; Domain Rating 0.326; referring domains 0.295; branded traffic 0.274; number of backlinks 0.218; ad traffic 0.216; URL rating 0.18; site pages 0.17. ~26% of brands had zero AI Overview mentions and were excluded. Authors: correlation is not causation; all correlations are "moderate to very weak"; AIO visibility manipulation has been demonstrated. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)
- Same Ahrefs article cites Seer Interactive (ChatGPT mentions): Google keywords 0.65; Bing organic keywords 0.56; Bing keywords 0.50; Google SERP features 0.41; DR 0.25; backlinks 0.10. And Kevin Indig: ChatGPT mentions vs brand search volume 0.334. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)
- Secondary reports of a Dec 2025 Ahrefs follow-up give YouTube mentions a ~0.737-0.740 correlation with AI brand visibility; primary not retrieved, so unverified. — [Alex Ly summary](https://www.alex-ly.com/en/blog/visibilite-marque-chatgpt-google-ai-etude-ahrefs)
- Ahrefs separately reports a 0.77 correlation between ranking for fan-out queries and being cited in AI Overviews, and ~0.04 for word count. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-to-rank-in-ai-overviews/)
- Wellows (vendor; 20.5M citations): 47.9% of citations came from domains under DA 40; 301 DA 90+ domains took 17.0% of citation share. — [Wellows](https://wellows.com/research/does-domain-authority-matter-for-ai-citations/)
- Semrush Reddit study: cited Reddit posts had median 5-8 upvotes; ~80% under 20 upvotes; semantic similarity to the answer (0.53-0.54) mattered, engagement did not. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddit-ai-search-visibility-study/)
- Semrush/Indig: 61.7% of citations are ghost citations (no brand name in answer), so link-level citation counts overstate brand visibility; mentions and citations must be tracked separately. — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/blog/the-ghost-citations-study/)
- Ahrefs: only 14.4% of ChatGPT responses triggered a web search, so "for many prompts, visibility depends on what the model already knows about a brand" (parametric memory formed from training-time mentions). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt)

### Inferences
- The consistent ordering across Ahrefs, Seer and Indig (mentions/search demand > authority metrics > raw backlinks) is the best available quantitative support for the "mentions are the new backlinks" claim, but no study has an experimental design, and brand size confounds everything (big brands get more mentions, more search volume, and more AI visibility).
- Because ~86% of ChatGPT answers do not search the web, the mention-volume effect likely operates through two channels: training-data frequency (parametric) and retrieval-time listicle/review presence.

### Gaps
- No controlled or longitudinal study found that increases a brand's third-party mentions and measures a subsequent change in AI recommendation rate.
- The Dec 2025 Ahrefs YouTube follow-up was not retrieved in primary form.

## KQ5: Do content licensing deals measurably shift citation share toward partners?

### Takeaway
Partial yes, platform-specific: Otterly/Press Ranger (June 2026, 129M citations) found OpenAI-licensed publishers earn 48% more ChatGPT citations per cited page (112% for OpenAI-exclusive publishers) and Ahrefs saw OpenAI partner publications rise from 7.5% to 15.8% of ChatGPT citations between March and July 2026, but Google-licensed publishers showed no AI Overviews premium and Perplexity licensees were at parity; the Tow Center found licensing did not improve citation accuracy.

### Cited Findings
- Otterly.ai + Press Ranger (Aug 20, 2026): 314 licensed publisher domains from 91 confirmed deals; June 2026 citation data (129.3M citations, 20M+ URLs) across 7 platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Google AI Overviews + other Alphabet surfaces). OpenAI-licensed publishers: 10.2 citations per cited page on ChatGPT vs 6.9 unlicensed (+48%); OpenAI cohort across all 7 platforms +46%; any licensing deal +51%; OpenAI-exclusive publishers +112% on ChatGPT but only ~+45% elsewhere. Google-licensed publishers cited at a slightly LOWER rate on AI Overviews; Google-only signatories no premium. Perplexity licensees at parity (Perplexity-only +6 points vs their away baseline). Copilot showed +61-85% for licensed publishers regardless of which company they signed with (scale effect). Caveats: scale confound (licensees are large outlets), unequal sample construction, vendor authorship. — [Otterly](https://insights.otterly.ai/ai-news-content-licensing-deals/)
- Ahrefs (Oct 2026): OpenAI publisher partners rose from 7.5% of ChatGPT citations in March 2026 to 15.8% in July, then 12.5% in late Sept 2026. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt)
- Tow Center (CJR, March 2025; 1,600 queries, 8 AI search tools): >60% of citation-retrieval attempts failed; Perplexity 37% error, Grok-3 94%; Gemini and Grok 3 produced more fabricated links than correct ones in 200 tests; tools cited syndicated copies instead of originals; "licensing deals with news sources did not guarantee accurate citation." — [CJR Tow Center](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php); [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/03/ai-search-engines-fail-to-produce-accurate-citations-in-over-60-of-tests/)
- WIRED test (reported ~April 1, 2026): ChatGPT, asked which products WIRED's reviewers recommend, linked to WIRED's buying guides but named picks that were not in them (e.g., LG QNED Evo Mini-LED instead of the TCL QM6K; AirPods Max 2 before WIRED had added them) — despite Condé Nast's content deal with OpenAI. Primary WIRED article not retrieved; details from secondary coverage. — [TechBuzz summary](https://techbuzz.ai/articles/chatgpt-fails-product-recommendation-test-hallucinates-wired-picks)
- Reddit deals: Reddit's AI licensing deals with Google (~$60M/yr) and OpenAI (~$70M/yr est.) were ~10% of its $1.3B revenue in FY2024 per Adweek; in Sept 2025 Reddit sought "deeper integration with Google's AI products" and dynamic pricing. Reddit sued Anthropic (June 2025) and Perplexity (Oct 2025) for unlicensed scraping. — [Slashdot/Adweek](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/02/14/0019213/); [Slashdot Sept 2025](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/09/22/0313234)
- OpenAI-Reddit partnership (May 16, 2024): OpenAI "will bring enhanced Reddit content to ChatGPT" via Reddit's Data API, real-time; OpenAI becomes a Reddit advertising partner; terms undisclosed. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-and-reddit-partnership/)
- Reddit's Q2 2025 earnings call cited Profound data that Reddit was the #1 most-cited source in AI models, "three times more frequently than Wikipedia" — a vendor figure. — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/?p=76442)
- Perplexity Publisher Program / Comet Plus: $42.5M pool funded by the $5/month Comet Plus subscription; publishers receive 80% of revenue; paid per page visits, citations in AI answers and agent actions; participating publishers undisclosed. — [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/perplexity-ai-rolls-out-new-revenue-sharing-program-for-publishers); [WebsiteBuilderExpert](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/news/perplexity-comet-plus/)
- Google Search Central "AI features and your website" (last updated Dec 10, 2025): "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary"; pages must be indexed and snippet-eligible; both features may use query fan-out, "issuing multiple related searches across subtopics and data sources"; recommends "helpful, reliable, people-first content". The page does not mention E-E-A-T. — [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

### Inferences
- The licensing effect appears real but concentrated on ChatGPT, where OpenAI controls retrieval ranking; Google's AI Overviews rank from its ordinary index, so licensing does not translate into citation share there. For consumer product recommendations, this means Condé Nast (WIRED, Ars), News Corp, Axel Springer (Business Insider) and Dotdash Meredith titles plausibly enjoy a ChatGPT citation advantage over Wirecutter (NYT, which is litigating against OpenAI) — but no study isolates review publishers by licensing status.
- Licensing buys retrieval access and link prominence, not accuracy: the WIRED and Tow Center results show partners' content can be cited and still misrepresented.

### Gaps
- No study found that measures the Reddit-Google or Reddit-OpenAI deals' effect on Reddit citation share before/after signing (Reddit's rise and fall seems driven by retrieval-policy changes, e.g., the Sept 2025 ChatGPT shift and 2026 decline, not by deal dates).
- OpenAI's own published description of how partner content is prioritized/attributed in ChatGPT search was not retrieved.

## KQ6: Counter-evidence: low-authority sites cited, fabricated sources, and citation churn/volatility

### Takeaway
The "wall" is porous and unstable: spam/programmatic sites took >14% of ChatGPT and ~11% of Perplexity citations in B2B prompts (June 2026), an obscure site (Toxigon) sat in ChatGPT's all-time top 10 in 2025, ~16% of cited sources in an academic audit showed signs of being AI-generated, and only ~25% of pages ChatGPT cites for a prompt reappear the next day — though the leading brand recommended for a prompt is far stickier (named on 82-89% of days).

### Cited Findings
- Low-authority sites: Toxigon.com (0.7% of all ChatGPT citations, 9th overall) appeared in Profound's Aug 2024-June 2025 ChatGPT top 10 alongside Reuters and Forbes. — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns)
- Spam/programmatic domains: >14% of ChatGPT citations and ~11% of Perplexity citations for B2B "best [category]" prompts pointed to programmatically generated sites, vs 0.16% for Google AI Overviews and 0.46% for Gemini (June 2026). — [Overthink Group](https://overthinkgroup.com/b2b-ai-citation-stats-2026-q2/)
- Wellows (20.5M citations): 47.9% of citations from DA<40 domains. — [Wellows](https://wellows.com/research/does-domain-authority-matter-for-ai-citations/)
- Allaham & Diakopoulos (AIES 2026): ~16% of sources cited by ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini and Perplexity showed evidence of AI generation. — [arXiv 2605.23684](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23684)
- Tow Center (March 2025): >60% failure rate; Gemini and Grok 3 provided more fabricated links than correct ones; Grok 3 pointed to 404 pages 154 times in 200 tests; paid tiers no better. — [CJR Tow Center](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php)
- Detailed.com / Glen Allsopp (Sept 23, 2026): 70,000+ responses, 1,300+ mostly commercial prompts checked daily for 28 days on ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. Only ~1/4 of pages ChatGPT cited for a prompt reappeared the next day (~1/2 for AI Overviews). Core domains (cited on >=80% of days) changed 14%/12% day to day; the tail changed 74%/61%. Probability of an identical domain set on two random days: 2.4% (ChatGPT), 0.3% (AIO). But the most frequently named brand appeared on 82% (ChatGPT) / 89% (AIO) of days and was named first on 50%/60% of days; 41% of ChatGPT prompts never produced the same brand set twice in the month (30% AIO). Only about half of ChatGPT prompts that named brands had a "core" brand at all. — [Detailed](https://detailed.com/ai-volatility/)
- SparkToro + Gumshoe.ai (Rand Fishkin; 2026): ~600 volunteers ran prompts ~3,000 times across ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI; fewer than 1 in 100 runs produced the same brand list and fewer than 1 in 1,000 the same order, yet the same top brands recurred per category (e.g., Bose or Sony for headphones). Primary write-up not retrieved; figures from secondary coverage. — [nohacks podcast summary](https://nohacks.co/guest/rand-fishkin); [digitrendz](https://digitrendz.blog/?p=127153)
- GetMentions (vendor; June 2026; 2,398 queries daily for a week; 530,875 citations): ChatGPT daily source churn 79.2% (median 82% per query); ChatGPT and Google AI Mode shed ~60% of sources overnight. — [GetMentions](https://www.getmentions.ai/blog/ai-citation-volatility-study)
- Digital Authority Partners (via Everything-PR; 1,127 URLs, three waves over six weeks): only 10.6% of URLs cited by AI engines persisted across a 28-day window. — [Everything-PR](https://everything-pr.com/the-citation-half-life-why-89-percent-of-ai-visibility-wins-are-gone-within-a-month)
- Semrush: ChatGPT's Reddit citations fell from ~60% of responses (early Aug 2025) to ~10% (mid-Sept 2025) and Wikipedia from ~55% to <20% within weeks, coinciding with Google's removal of the num=100 parameter (~Sept 11, 2025); Semrush's Sergei Rogulin suspects OpenAI deliberately reduced over-citation of certain sites. Post-shift ChatGPT winners: PRNewswire, Forbes, Medium; losers: Reddit, Wikipedia, TechRadar. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai/)
- Uberti-Bona Marin et al. (arXiv Sept 16, 2026; 2,528 real commercial-advice queries, 1,536 product responses; ChatGPT and Gemini chatbot+API, AI Overviews): recommended products often changed across repeated requests; ChatGPT and Gemini shared only 5.4% of cited domains on average for the same query and had zero domain overlap in 76.7% of comparisons; API outputs overlapped only 12.0% (ChatGPT) / 14.8% (Gemini) with their own consumer interfaces; ChatGPT expressed a first-person product preference in 79% of recommending responses vs 7% Gemini and 2% AI Overviews. — [arXiv 2609.18729](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18729)
- Chudi.dev case: a DR-25 site reports earning ~1,500 ChatGPT citations by targeting specific query axes (single-site anecdote). — [chudi.dev](https://chudi.dev/blog/why-ai-isnt-citing-your-website)

### Inferences
- URL-level citations are a flow, not a stock; domain-level and especially brand-level recommendations are much more stable. A brand that is the category leader in AI answers tends to stay so (named on 80-90% of days), while the second-tier shortlist is reshuffled almost daily — meaning absence from third-party sources does not permanently exclude a brand, but presence does not guarantee a stable slot either.
- ChatGPT and Perplexity's willingness to cite programmatic/spam sites (and ChatGPT's 2025 Toxigon anomaly) shows retrieval relevance can override authority, which cuts both ways for new brands: a well-structured new page can get cited, and so can a fake one.
- Cross-engine source overlap near zero (5.4%) means "the citation economy" is really several loosely coupled economies; a brand cited heavily in Perplexity (G2/Reddit-heavy) may be invisible in Gemini (vendor-site-heavy).

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed longitudinal study of month-over-month citation churn specifically for consumer "best X" prompts; Detailed.com is the closest (commercial prompts, 28 days, two platforms).
- The SSRN "citation half-life" preprint (median 3.4 weeks ChatGPT vs 5.8 weeks Perplexity) was not retrieved and is a single-author preprint built on vendor datasets.

## KQ7: How do AI referral traffic and conversions distribute between publishers and brands?

### Takeaway
AI assistants send very little traffic relative to what they crawl (Cloudflare: Anthropic ~38,000:1, OpenAI ~1,100:1, Perplexity ~100-200:1, Google ~5:1 crawl-to-referral in mid-2025, with most ratios falling through 2026), but what they do send increasingly goes to brands rather than publishers: after OpenAI made brand links more prominent on May 7, 2026, Similarweb saw the share of ChatGPT referrals landing on brand homepages jump from ~26-32% to ~60%, and Adobe reports AI-referred retail traffic converting 42% better than non-AI traffic as of March 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Cloudflare Radar (reported Aug 2025, July 2025 data): crawl-to-referral ratios — Anthropic ~38,066:1 (down from ~286,930:1 in Jan 2025), OpenAI ~1,091:1 (from 1,217), Perplexity ~100-200:1 with spikes >700:1 in March 2025, Google ~4-5:1. Secondary reports put Anthropic at ~4,580:1 (June 2026), ~1,917:1 (July 2026), OpenAI 848:1, Perplexity 186:1 (June 2026). Figures are from secondary compilations of Cloudflare Radar and are not mutually consistent; dates in some blogs appear mislabeled. — [SEOmator](https://seomator.com/blog/crawl-to-refer-ratio-ai-crawlers-llm-bots); [TechnologyChecker](https://technologychecker.io/blog/robots-txt-ai-crawlers-blocking-report); [Nobori](https://nobori.ai/blog/crawl-to-refer-ratio-ai-crawler-traffic-b2b-2026)
- Similarweb (May-June 2026): before May 7, 2026, ~26-32% of ChatGPT referrals landed on a brand's homepage; after OpenAI made brand links more prominent in answers, the share jumped to ~60% and stayed there; total ChatGPT referrals rose 157.7% week over week. Similarweb's post URL returned 404 on fetch; figures from search-indexed summaries of the Similarweb blog. — [Similarweb (via summaries)](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/); [SEO Francisco summary](https://seofrancisco.com/insights/chatgpt-referral-traffic-clicky-future/)
- Similarweb: ChatGPT's share of generative-AI website visits fell from ~76% (June 2025) to ~53% (May 2026); Gemini 27.3%, Claude 8.9% (June 11, 2026 reading). — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)
- Adobe Digital Insights (reported April 2026; >1 trillion visits to US retail sites; 5,000-consumer survey March 2026): AI traffic to US retailers up 393% YoY in Q1 2026; AI-referred traffic converted 42% MORE often than non-AI traffic as of March 2026 (vs 38% LOWER in March 2025); revenue per visit 37% higher; AI visitors spent 48% longer and viewed 13% more pages; 39% of consumers used AI assistants for shopping, 66% trusted AI to give accurate results. Primary Adobe report not retrieved. — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/04/23/ecommerce-trends-ai-key-conversion-metric/); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/364733/ai-traffic-us-retailers-jumps-q1-agentic-shoppers-outspend-humans)
- Similarweb analysis of ChatGPT citations (Nov 2024, via TechCrunch): news publishers that block OpenAI's crawler still get cited via syndicated copies; Tow Center found ChatGPT invents or misattributes citations regardless of licensing status. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/29/study-of-chatgpt-citations-makes-dismal-reading-for-publishers/)
- RTINGS paywalled its full test data in March 2026 citing declining Google traffic and uncredited AI scraping — an example of a heavily cited third-party reviewer withdrawing content from the open web. — [Wikipedia: RTINGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTINGS)
- Perplexity's Comet Plus program is the only major platform mechanism found that pays publishers per citation/visit (80% of a $5/month subscription, $42.5M pool). — [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/perplexity-ai-rolls-out-new-revenue-sharing-program-for-publishers)

### Inferences
- The citation economy and the referral economy are decoupling: third-party publishers supply the majority of citations for discovery prompts (KQ2) but, after May 2026, brands receive the majority of ChatGPT click-throughs. Publishers are therefore being "read" far more than they are "visited", which is the economic pressure behind paywalls (RTINGS), licensing deals and lawsuits (NYT, Reddit v. Perplexity/Anthropic).
- High AI-referral conversion rates (Adobe +42%) imply that when a brand does make the shortlist, the downstream value per visit is above average, raising the stakes of third-party shortlist inclusion.

### Gaps
- No source found that splits total AI referral traffic between publisher domains and brand/retailer domains across all platforms; Similarweb's homepage-share figure is the closest proxy and only covers ChatGPT.
- Cloudflare's own primary posts were not fetched; all ratios are from secondary compilations and should be re-verified against Cloudflare Radar before quoting precisely.
