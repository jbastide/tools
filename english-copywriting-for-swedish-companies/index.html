Why isn’t good Swedish English enough for American customers?

Good Swedish English is correct, but correct copy doesn’t sell by itself. Sweden ranks 8th of 123 countries and regions in the EF English Proficiency Index 2025, so the mistakes are rarely grammatical. They are in word choice, tone and details that an American reader notices within seconds, and that make the company seem far away.

What is the difference between translation and copywriting?

A translation moves the text from Swedish to English. Copywriting starts with the buyer instead: what an American customer searches for, what they want to know and what makes them get in touch. That is why good English sales copy is rarely a sentence-by-sentence version of the Swedish.

A Swedish company page often starts with the company: when it was founded, where it is and what it stands for. An American buyer first wants to know what you do for them, what it costs and how fast it goes. The rest can come further down, as proof.

So a rewrite changes four things more than the words: the order, the headline, the proof and the call to action. I’m a copywriter, not a translator. If you need a certified translation of contracts, manuals or legal texts, a translation agency is the right choice.

We get visitors from the US but no sales. Can the copy be the problem?

Yes, often. When American visitors reach the page but don’t get in touch, it is usually because the page doesn’t answer what they want to know, or because it doesn’t look like it was written for them. That can be checked before a single line is rewritten.

An American buyer considering a Swedish supplier looks for these things, roughly in this order:

  1. Do you sell to the US at all? Say it in plain words: delivery, delivery time and who handles customs and shipping.
  2. What does it cost, in dollars? A price or a price range. “Request a quote” with no number reads as expensive.
  3. Who do I talk to, and when? Opening hours in US time, and a phone number or booking link that works from there.
  4. Has anyone like me bought from you? American customers, or standards and certifications that apply in the US.
  5. What do I do now? One call to action, not five.

Sometimes the fix isn’t more words. It’s one fewer form field, one fewer step, or one sentence that makes people flinch. I look for the place where a single change does the most to what a new customer costs. Because I also run ad campaigns, I’ve watched, with other people’s money on the line, which words made strangers click and which made them leave.

Why not let ChatGPT translate the text?

AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude translate Swedish text into correct English in seconds, and that makes a good first draft. The problem is that your competitors use the same tools, so the copy ends up with the same rhythm and the same words. What the tool doesn’t know is what your particular American customers want to know, and which of ten possible claims should come first.

I use AI myself, as an assistant and not as the one who decides. Everything published in your name goes through me. Copy that sounds like a person, and dares to say something concrete, is one of the few advantages that can’t be copied in a minute.

What rules apply to advertising aimed at customers in the US?

Advertising in the United States must, according to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), be truthful and non-deceptive, and the advertiser must have evidence to back up its claims. Under the FTC’s Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255), testimonials and endorsements must reflect the honest opinion or experience of the person giving them. The principle resembles section 10 of the Swedish Marketing Act, which prohibits incorrect and misleading claims.

For Swedish copy being rewritten for the US, that means in practice:

  • Claims that can be measured, such as “30% faster”, have to be backed by evidence you hold.
  • Customer quotes have to be real, and the customer has to have said what is quoted.
  • Industries such as health, finance and food have their own rules. Your lawyer knows those better than I do.

This is not legal advice. I write so the claims can be substantiated. You and your lawyer decide what the company may say.

FAQ

Is the copy written in American or British English?

The copy is written in American English and is meant for customers in the United States. That covers spelling, word choice, dates, measurements and tone.

Can copy have an edge and still stay within the rules?

Yes. The edge is in how you speak and how much of the truth you dare to say, not in the claims. Honest copy with a pulse can be written even in industries where every claim has to be substantiated.

Do you promise that new copy will increase sales?

No. Nobody can know in advance how a piece of copy will perform. What can be done is to change what is most likely costing customers, measure the result and change it again. If ads run to the page, the difference shows up in cost per inquiry.
