What does a Google Ads consultant for lead generation do?

A Google Ads consultant for lead generation makes sure a company’s search ads are shown to people looking for exactly that service, and that as many of them as possible get in touch at a cost the company makes money on. The measure is cost per inquiry and cost per new customer, not clicks or impressions.

At Copy for Originals, managing a Google Ads account includes:

  • Keywords and negative keywords. Which searches the ads should show on, and which they should never show on.
  • Ad copy. Written by a copywriter, from how customers describe the problem themselves. Swedish-language copy is translated with AI and proofread by Swedish speakers before it is published.
  • Measurement. An inquiry counts as an inquiry. Page views and clicks don’t.
  • Bidding and budget. A cap on what an inquiry may cost, set from what a new customer is worth.
  • The landing page. What to change in the copy, the form and the call to action so more visitors get in touch.
  • A monthly report. What the campaigns cost, what they produced and what we learned.

How do you stop the bleeding in a Google Ads account?

You stop the bleeding in a Google Ads account by first turning off what costs money without producing inquiries, and only then building anything new. In practice that is six checks, and most of them can be done in the first week.

  1. Search terms. Read what people actually typed when the ads were shown, and add the irrelevant searches as negative keywords. The Negative Keyword Finder sorts the report for you in the browser.
  2. Auto-applied recommendations. Turn all of them off. Otherwise Google can add keywords and change bidding without anyone approving it.
  3. The location option. Switch from “Presence or interest”, which is Google’s default, to “Presence”. With the default, the ads can also be shown to people who have only shown interest in the area. With “Presence”, they are shown to people who are likely to be located there, or regularly are.
  4. Networks. Turn off the Display Network and Search Partners in Search campaigns, so the budget goes to searches on Google.
  5. Conversions. Only real inquiries, meaning submitted forms and calls longer than a minute, should count as primary conversions. Page views, clicks and scrolls shouldn’t steer bidding.
  6. Keywords that have had their chance. A keyword that has cost three times what an inquiry is allowed to cost, without producing one, is 95% likely not to be bad luck. Pause it. The rule is explained in the PPC Gumball Rule.

The account is a mess. Should we clean it up or start over?

It is usually better to clean up the existing Google Ads account than to open a new one. The account holds the history: which searches cost money, which produced inquiries and what has already been tried. That history is the fastest way to avoid paying for the same lesson twice.

What I do is build a new, simple structure inside the account: one campaign per service, one landing page per campaign, and the old campaigns paused instead of deleted, so the numbers are still there to compare against.

A new account is really only needed when the company doesn’t own the old one, for example when a previous agency set it up in its own name and won’t hand it over. So one rule applies from day one: your company owns the Google Ads account and has Admin access. I work inside your account, the ad spend is paid directly to Google, and if we stop working together you keep everything.

Why doesn’t our company show up on Google?

A company doesn’t show up on Google for one of three reasons: its website doesn’t rank in the regular search results, it has no complete Google Business Profile for the map results, or it isn’t advertising on the searches its customers make. Google Ads solves the third: the ad can appear above the regular results for the searches you choose, in the area you choose.

The fear of being invisible online is reasonable. Someone who searches for a service and doesn’t find your company finds another one, and you never learn that it happened. But visibility isn’t the goal. An ad shown on the wrong searches is visibility too, and it costs money.

If you already advertise and the ads still don’t show, the usual causes are a daily budget that is too low, bids that are too low, keywords almost nobody searches for, or ads that haven’t been approved. The PPC Troubleshooter walks through the causes one at a time. Googling your own company is not a good test: your own searches add ad impressions without clicks and affect the statistics. Use Google’s Ad preview and diagnosis tool instead. It shows whether the ad appears for a given search, and why not.

I work on both ads and search engine optimization (SEO). Ads give visibility as soon as the campaign is running, but only while the budget is on, which is why they have to pay for themselves in inquiries. SEO takes longer but stays when the ad budget is switched off.

We get clicks but no inquiries. What’s wrong?

When a Google Ads account produces clicks but no inquiries, there are either too few clicks to judge, the wrong searches, or a landing page that doesn’t answer what the visitor searched for. Start with the math: if 2.5% of visitors normally get in touch, zero inquiries from 100 clicks is still bad luck about 8 times in 100, but after 120 clicks it is 95% likely that something is wrong.

If there are enough clicks and the searches are the right ones, the problem is on the page, and this is where copywriting makes the difference. A good landing page finishes the thought the search started:

  • The headline repeats what the person searched for. If the ad promises “a quote within 24 hours”, the page says the same thing, at the top.
  • The page answers what people want to know before they get in touch. Roughly what it costs, how long it takes and who they will talk to.
  • One call to action. A form with as few fields as possible, or a phone number somebody actually answers.
  • Claims that can be checked. Years, counts, places and names, instead of “high quality” and “extensive experience”.

Sometimes the fix isn’t more words. It’s one fewer form field, or one fewer step. I write the ads and the page copy and run the campaigns, so one person is responsible for the whole path from search to inquiry. The changes to the page itself are made by whoever maintains your website. From me you get the copy and a clear description of what to change.

What is digital marketing (digital marknadsföring), and where does Google Ads fit in?

Digital marketing, “digital marknadsföring” in Swedish, is all marketing done in digital channels: search ads, search engine optimization (SEO), social media, email, content and the company’s own website. Google Ads is the part of digital marketing where a company pays to appear in Google’s search results at the moment someone searches for what the company sells.

How are cookies, consent and GDPR handled in the measurement?

Under chapter 9, section 28 of the Swedish Electronic Communications Act (2022:482), information may be stored on or read from a visitor’s device, for example with cookies, only if the visitor has been told the purpose and consents. Google also requires advertisers to obtain valid consent from visitors in the EEA before personal data is used for advertising, and to pass that consent on to Google. Measurement in a Swedish Google Ads account therefore has to be built to follow the visitor’s choice.

  • Supervision. The Swedish Post and Telecom Authority (PTS) supervises the Electronic Communications Act. The Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) supervises the processing of personal data that follows, under the GDPR.
  • Google’s consent mode. Two of the signals concern advertising: ad_user_data, consent for sending user data to Google for advertising purposes, and ad_personalization, consent for personalized advertising.
  • What I do. I check that conversion measurement follows the visitor’s consent and that Google only gets what it needs to count an inquiry. When an inquiry becomes a customer in your own system, that can be imported into Google Ads afterwards as an offline conversion.

This describes how measurement is set up and is not legal advice. The consent banner and your processing of personal data are your own responsibility.

How soon can you tell whether Google Ads is working?

It is measured in clicks, not days. If 5% of visitors are expected to get in touch, the first inquiry should have arrived within 60 clicks. If 60 clicks have produced zero inquiries, it is 95% likely that something is wrong, and it is time to troubleshoot instead of waiting.

How does VAT on Google Ads work for a Swedish company?

Advertisers with a billing address in the EU are billed by Google Ireland Ltd. According to Google, VAT is not included in Google Ads costs when the business is in an EU country other than Ireland. The company instead calculates and reports Swedish VAT itself in its VAT return, which the Swedish Tax Agency calls “omvänd betalningsskyldighet” (reverse charge). Ask whoever keeps your books how to report it.
