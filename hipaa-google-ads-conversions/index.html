HIPAA-compliant conversion tracking for Google Ads: the options compared

HIPAA-compliant conversion tracking for Google Ads is a way for a healthcare practice to tell Google Ads which ad clicks became leads or patients without sending protected health information (PHI) to Google, which does not sign a business associate agreement (BAA) for Google Ads. This guide compares every option we found as of October 2026, from healthcare tracking platforms such as Freshpaint to free offline conversion imports, with prices, setup effort, and whether a practice without an IT department can realistically run each one. This guide is educational only and is not legal advice. Confirm any tracking setup with a healthcare attorney or your compliance officer before you use it.

By Copy for Originals · Last updated October 5, 2026 · Sources accessed October 5, 2026

Short answer

Every compliant option works the same way at the end: Google receives only the ad click ID, a timestamp, an optional value and a neutral conversion name, never anything that identifies the patient or the care they asked about. What differs is who does the work and what it costs. A Google Ads offline conversion import is free but takes staff time. Managed healthcare platforms such as Curve and Ours Privacy do the setup for you, from roughly $900 to a few thousand dollars a month. Freshpaint and the enterprise CDPs typically cost tens of thousands of dollars a year. For a practice whose ad budget doesn’t justify those costs, Manual CPC judged by new-patient growth is a sound alternative.

Why can’t a healthcare practice use the standard Google Ads conversion tag?

A healthcare practice can’t safely use the standard Google Ads conversion tag because the tag sends Google the visitor’s IP address, page URLs and browser identifiers, and Google does not sign a business associate agreement (BAA) for Google Ads. Without a BAA, no PHI may reach Google.

  • Google Analytics: Google’s help page says Google “does not offer Business Associate Agreements in connection with this service.” Primary Google Analytics Help
  • Google Cloud does sign a BAA, and Cloud Run is a covered service, but Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager are not on the covered list. Primary Google Cloud
  • The HHS tracking bulletin is narrower than it was. On June 20, 2024, a federal court in American Hospital Association v. Becerra vacated the part of the HHS bulletin that treated an IP address plus a visit to a public health page as PHI, and HHS withdrew its appeal in August 2024. The rest of the bulletin stands: tracking on login-protected pages such as patient portals, and anything a patient types about their health, are still treated as PHI. Law-firm summaries AHA, Holland & Knight
  • Lawsuits don’t depend on HIPAA alone. Pixel class actions often use state wiretap laws. Sutter Health’s $21.5 million settlement over Google Analytics and Meta Pixel on its patient-portal login page received final approval in March 2026. ClassAction.org

Which option fits a practice without an IT department?

A practice without an IT department has three realistic paths: pay a managed healthcare platform to do the work, run a free offline conversion import with existing staff, or skip conversion-based bidding and run Manual CPC. The right choice depends on how the tracking cost compares with the ad budget.

  • Managed platform (Curve, Ours Privacy). The least work for the practice and the highest cost. At Curve’s $899 a month, a practice spending $3,000 a month on Google Ads would pay about 30% of its ad budget again for tracking; a practice spending $15,000 a month would pay about 6%.
  • Offline conversion import. Free, and practical for a front desk that can update a spreadsheet. It needs the website form to capture the click ID, which many scheduling and EHR booking widgets can’t do. See the steps below.
  • Manual CPC. No conversion data sent to Google at all. Results are judged by new-patient growth against ad spend. See Can Manual CPC work without conversion tracking?

Server-side GTM and the enterprise CDPs need a developer or analytics consultant to set up and maintain, so they fit practices that already work with one.

How does a Google Ads offline conversion import work?

A Google Ads offline conversion import records a conversion by uploading the ad click ID (GCLID) and the conversion time, so Google learns which clicks produced patients without receiving any patient data. Google doesn’t charge for it. Primary Google Ads Help

  1. Turn on auto-tagging in Google Ads (Admin, Account settings), so every ad click carries a GCLID in the landing page URL.
  2. Add a hidden field to the website contact or booking form that saves the GCLID with the lead.
  3. Create an import conversion action with a neutral name such as “Qualified lead” or “New patient”, and wait 4 to 6 hours before the first upload.
  4. When a lead becomes a patient, add a row with only the GCLID, the conversion name, the conversion time and an optional value.
  5. Upload by CSV, a scheduled Google Sheets upload or Google’s Data Manager, within 90 days of the click. Upload on a steady schedule, daily if you use Smart Bidding. Imported conversions take 24 to 48 hours to appear.

The hard step is the second one: if the booking tool can’t store the GCLID, there is nothing to upload. Also keep in mind the open question in What this guide could not verify: Google’s sensitive-category rule names enhanced conversions and store sales, and we could not confirm whether it also covers click-ID-only imports.

Can a healthcare practice use enhanced conversions?

Enhanced conversions are risky for a healthcare practice because Google’s own policy excludes health-related conversions from them. Google’s customer data policy says conversions in sensitive categories, which include “health or medical information,” can’t be used for measurement in enhanced conversions or store sales uploads. Primary Google Ads Help

Enhanced conversions send a hashed email address or phone number. The FTC has said that hashing does not make data anonymous. FTC, July 2024

Some healthcare tracking platforms, including Curve and Freshpaint, describe enhanced conversions with PHI removed in their own materials. If a vendor offers it, ask the vendor how it fits Google’s policy, and have your counsel review the answer before turning it on.

How do you track phone calls from Google Ads under HIPAA?

To track phone calls from Google Ads under HIPAA, use a call tracking service that signs a BAA and send Google only the call conversion, never call recordings, transcripts or caller details. A BAA with the call tracking vendor does not extend to Google.

What changed for Google Ads offline conversion imports in June 2026?

Starting June 15, 2026, Google moved offline conversion imports and enhanced conversions for leads to the Data Manager API and blocked them in the Google Ads API for new integrations. Primary Google Ads Help

Manual CSV and Google Sheets uploads still work, so the change matters most to practices that wanted a custom API connection. Vendors with existing integrations, such as RudderStack, say their connections are unaffected.

Can Manual CPC work without conversion tracking?

Manual CPC can work well without conversion tracking when a practice judges Google Ads by the relationship between ad spend and new-patient growth over time, instead of by a tracked conversion count. Smart Bidding strategies such as Maximize conversions need conversion data to bid; Manual CPC doesn’t.

In Copy for Originals’ client work, a Manual CPC campaign with high-six-figure spend held its own against the same campaign’s results on Maximize conversions the previous year, once the benchmark was the practice’s actual new-patient growth rather than a tracked “conversion.” That is one client’s experience, not a controlled study, but it shows the tradeoff is about measurement, not about whether a practice can compete.

To run Google Ads this way, track monthly ad spend next to new patients from the practice management system, ask new patients how they found the practice, and watch how new-patient numbers move when spend goes up or down. HIPAA Google Ads conversion tracking FAQ

Is Google Ads HIPAA compliant?

No. Google does not sign a business associate agreement (BAA) for Google Ads, so a healthcare practice can use Google Ads only if no protected health information reaches Google. In practice that means sending only the ad click ID, a timestamp, an optional value and a neutral conversion name.

Is Freshpaint the only HIPAA-compliant option for Google Ads conversions?

No. Curve and Ours Privacy are healthcare tracking platforms that compete directly with Freshpaint, enterprise CDPs such as Segment, RudderStack, Tealium and Piwik PRO offer a BAA on their top tiers, server-side Google Tag Manager can run on a host that signs a BAA, and Google Ads offline conversion imports are free.

What is the cheapest HIPAA-compliant way to track Google Ads conversions?

A Google Ads offline conversion import that sends only the click ID is free. The practice captures the GCLID in its website form, then uploads the click ID, a neutral conversion name and the conversion time by CSV or Google Sheets within 90 days of the click. The cost is staff time, not a subscription.

Can a healthcare practice run Google Ads without conversion tracking?

Yes. A practice can run Manual CPC and judge Google Ads by the relationship between ad spend and new-patient growth over time instead of by a tracked conversion count. Smart Bidding strategies such as Maximize conversions need conversion data, but Manual CPC does not.
