How this site makes money

Copy for Originals pays for its free tools and guides mainly through client services. Guides that compare tools may contain affiliate links, each one marked, and a commission never decides a ranking. The tools that read your data carry no analytics, ads or affiliate links.

By Copy for Originals · Published October 20, 2026 · Last updated October 20, 2026

How does this site make money?

Mainly through Copy for Originals’ client services: Google Ads management, copywriting, and SEO and AEO. The tools and guides are free, and they are how most clients find us. Guides that compare tools may also contain affiliate links, which can pay us a commission when a reader buys.

What is an affiliate link, and how are they marked?

An affiliate link is a link to a product that pays us a commission if you buy through it. It costs you nothing extra. Every page with an affiliate link says so in its first lines and in its footer, and every affiliate link is followed by the words “paid link”, which the FTC treats as a clear label, and carries rel="sponsored" so search engines can tell it apart.

Clicking one takes you to the vendor’s site, which may set its own cookie to credit the sale. This site sets no cookie for it.

Do affiliate commissions change which tool you recommend?

No. We set a comparison’s ranking from its data before joining any affiliate program, and a commission never changes it. Tools that pay nothing, including the free and open-source ones, get the same depth. We take no sponsored placements or paid reviews, and vendors may correct facts before we publish, never verdicts.

  • Every comparison says at the top what it rests on: the vendors’ own pages, coded user reviews, or tests we ran ourselves.
  • Every comparison shows the date its features, prices and reviews were checked. We refresh them every six months, or after a tool’s major release.
  • Checks run by an AI agent are labelled as agent-run, with the date, the inputs and the raw outputs.

Are there ads on this site?

Not as of October 20, 2026. If display ads are added, they will appear only on guides, only after you accept advertising cookies, and never inside tables, results or calculators. This page and the cookie banner will say so first.

Which pages never carry analytics, ads or affiliate links?

Every page where you type, paste or upload data: the Google Ads report tools, the calculators and the checklists. They load no Google Analytics, no ads and no affiliate links, and most of them have a Content-Security-Policy that blocks the page from sending your data to any server.

Guides with no inputs use Google Analytics only if you accept cookies. Cookie settings change that choice at any time.

How do I report a link or a ranking that looks wrong?

Write through the contact page with the page address. A wrong fact is corrected and the page’s “Last updated” date changes with it.
